# AudaCity overview

## Brand

AudaCity is the cryptocurrency brand associated with Dacity Coin in the *Grand Theft Auto VI* universe. Its identity is built around a bold A monogram and a neon palette of electric blue, violet, and magenta.

## In-game presence

AudaCity's verified promotional identity and live Dacity Coin presence establish its place in the game's digital-currency landscape. The official visual reference is available at [`../assets/logo.png`](../assets/logo.png).

## Project scope

This project documents the official in-game identity, currency naming, verified metadata, live network deployment, and visual presentation.
